FAQ

Common Questions

What if my phone number has changed?

The lookup uses the number registered at the time of policy purchase. If you've changed your number, call us at +91 99455 09306 and we'll update your record.

Why do I need both phone and date of birth?

Phone number alone isn't sufficient — anyone who knows your number could look up your policy. Date of birth is a second factor to protect your privacy without adding OTP friction.

My details are correct but nothing shows. Why?

Your policy may be registered under a slightly different number, or the record may not yet have been imported. Contact our team and we'll look it up manually within the hour.

Can I download my policy document here?

Not in the current version. This tool shows coverage details only. Policy PDF download, nominee updates, and claim history will be added in a future update.

Is this tool secure?

Yes. Lookups are rate-limited to prevent enumeration attacks, the page runs over HTTPS, and only plan-level information is shown — no financial, payment, or medical data.

My policy shows Expired or Lapsed. What next?

Call us immediately at +91 99455 09306 or email corporate.blr@rexabroking.com. Our renewal team will guide you through reinstating cover without a break in protection.
