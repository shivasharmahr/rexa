Frequently Asked Questions

Common inquiries regarding commercial risk and liability coverage.

Does Fire & Special Perils cover Business Interruption (FLOP)?
No, Business Interruption (also known as Fire Loss of Profit or FLOP) is typically an add-on or a separate policy. A standard Fire policy covers the physical damage to the asset, while a FLOP policy covers the fixed costs and net profit lost during the time it takes to rebuild and resume operations.
Who exactly is protected under a corporate D&O policy?
A Directors & Officers (D&O) policy protects the personal assets of corporate directors, officers, and upper management against claims made by shareholders, employees, regulatory bodies, or other third parties alleging wrongful acts in managing the company.
What is the difference between Cyber Liability and Tech E&O?
Cyber Liability covers first-party and third-party costs arising from a data breach or cyberattack on your own systems (e.g. ransomware, notification costs). Tech E&O (Errors & Omissions) covers third-party financial loss if your software or technology product fails to perform as promised for a client.
