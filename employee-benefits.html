Frequently Asked Questions

Common inquiries from HR leaders regarding Group Health Insurance.

What is the minimum group size for a GMC policy?
Typically, a Group Mediclaim (GMC) policy requires a minimum of 7 to 10 employees to constitute a valid group under IRDAI guidelines, depending on the specific insurer's underwriting rules. We help startups and small businesses find the right entry point.
Are pre-existing diseases covered immediately?
Yes. Unlike retail health insurance which has waiting periods (usually 2-4 years) for pre-existing diseases, a properly structured corporate GMC policy waives these waiting periods, offering Day-1 coverage for all employees.
Can employees upgrade their coverage limits?
Yes, we highly recommend integrating a Voluntary Super Top-Up option. This allows the company to provide a base cover (e.g., ₹3 Lakhs), and employees can choose to pay a small premium from their own pocket to increase their limit (e.g., up to ₹10 Lakhs) for added security.
Are group health premiums tax deductible?
Yes. The premiums paid by an employer for the health insurance of its employees are generally considered a deductible business expense under Section 37(1) of the Income Tax Act, 1961, as it is an expense incurred wholly and exclusively for the purposes of the business.
