Privacy Policy

This explains what information Rexa Insurance Broking Services Pvt. Ltd. collects through this website, why, and how it's protected.

Last updated: 26 July 2026

1. Who we are

Rexa Insurance Broking Services Pvt. Ltd. ("Rexa", "we", "us", "our") is an IRDAI-licensed direct insurance broker (License No. 611, Life & General) headquartered at G4, Swathi Court, 22/43 Vijayaraghava Road, T Nagar, Chennai – 600017, with a branch office at 2nd Floor, 409, ITI Layout 3rd Phase, Phase 3, Nayanda Halli, Bengaluru, Karnataka 560039. This policy covers the website www.rexabroking.com and any forms hosted on it.

2. Information we collect

We only collect what you choose to give us. Depending on how you use this site, that may include:

  • Contact & enquiry details — name, company name, work email, phone number, the type of cover you're asking about, and any message you type into our enquiry form.
  • Policy & risk information — if you later share details for a quote or renewal (e.g. asset values, employee headcount, existing coverage), we collect only what's needed to place or service that cover.
  • Technical data — standard web request data such as browser type and IP address, generated automatically when you load the site or submit a form.

We do not use advertising trackers, third-party analytics cookies, or sell any data to third parties. Our on-site assistant ("Raksha") runs entirely in your browser to route you to the right page — anything you type into it is not transmitted to us or any server.

3. How we use it

  • To respond to your enquiry and follow up with a quote, comparison, or advisory call.
  • To place, renew, or service an insurance policy on your behalf, including liaising with insurers on claims.
  • To meet record-keeping and disclosure obligations under IRDAI regulations for insurance intermediaries.
  • To improve this website and communicate service updates or renewal reminders relevant to your existing cover.

We do not use your information for unrelated marketing without your consent, and you can ask us to stop non-essential communications at any time.

4. Sharing & third parties

We share information only where necessary to deliver the service you've asked for:

  • Insurers & reinsurers — to obtain quotes, place cover, and process claims.
  • Form processing — enquiry form submissions are relayed to our inbox via FormSubmit, a third-party form-to-email service; message content passes through their infrastructure in transit.
  • Spam protection — our contact form uses Google reCAPTCHA to filter automated submissions.
  • Fonts — this site loads typefaces from Google Fonts, which may log basic request data (e.g. IP address) as part of serving that request.
  • Regulators & law enforcement — where legally required, including IRDAI and courts of competent jurisdiction.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

5. Cookies & local storage

This site uses your browser's local storage (not tracking cookies) to remember a small number of on-site preferences — for example, whether you've dismissed a promotional banner, or whether you've already seen the Raksha assistant's intro message. These values stay on your device, aren't linked to your identity, and aren't used for advertising or cross-site tracking.

6. Data retention

We retain enquiry and policy-related information for as long as needed to service your policy and to meet the record-retention periods required under applicable IRDAI regulations and Indian law, after which it is securely deleted or anonymised.

7. Your rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and applicable Indian law, you can ask us to:

  • Confirm what personal data we hold about you and access a copy of it.
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Withdraw consent for non-essential communications, or ask us to erase your data, subject to our regulatory record-keeping obligations as a licensed broker.

To exercise any of these, email us at info@rexabroking.com and we'll respond within a reasonable time.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards to protect the information you share with us. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we encourage you to avoid sending highly sensitive information (such as full financial account numbers) over unencrypted channels like plain email.

9. Children's privacy

This site is intended for businesses and adults seeking insurance services. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will always reflect the latest version.
