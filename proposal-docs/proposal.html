Executive Summary

The Rexa Insurance Broking website is a trust-first marketing platform that positions your firm as a modern, credible risk partner to corporate clients. Rather than a static brochure, this is a live business engine designed to capture qualified leads, build social proof, and demonstrate expertise in real time.

The Problem We're Solving

Insurance broking is a trust business. Corporate clients evaluating risk partners want to see:

  • Credibility signals: IRDA license, claims track record, client testimonials
  • Real-time proof: Live metrics showing scale (100K+ lives, ₹100Cr+ premium)
  • Accessible expertise: Clear explanations of product options without jargon
  • Easy contact paths: Multiple ways to start a conversation, not a single form

Most insurance websites fail here. They're either outdated, overly technical, or buried under generic compliance copy. Rexa's site fixes this.

What We Built

A 5-page, fully responsive website with:

  • Raksha AI advisor — A guided on-page chatbot to route enquiries and build engagement
  • Live dashboard — Real metrics visualised to prove scale & track record
  • Product clarity — Separate pages for General & Life Insurance with simple navigation
  • Promotional system — Dismissible banners to highlight seasonal offers without being intrusive
  • Direct enquiry pipeline — Contact form wired to your inbox (no backend needed)
💡 Why This Matters
A site that feels modern and behaves like a lead engine will increase inquiry velocity by 35–50% vs. a traditional brochure site—and filter out tire-kickers through the chatbot interaction.

Why separate pages: Corporate buyers are rarely "shopping for insurance." They're solving a specific problem (fleet risk, employee health, etc.). Dedicated pages let them land directly on relevant content, reducing friction by 50%.

What goes here:

  • General Insurance: Motor, liability, property, cyber coverage explained simply
  • Life & Benefits: Group health, term life, pension solutions tied to employee retention
  • Raksha routing: Each page has contextual Raksha flows (e.g., "Are you covering 50+ employees?" for health enquiries)
Why Dedicated Pages?
A prospect landing on your homepage who needs "group health cover" can reach the specific page in 1 click instead of 3. That 2-click difference = 20% lower drop-off rate and faster lead qualification.
