Conversion Rate Optimization: Every Feature Reduces Friction

Each feature was chosen to reduce friction at a specific bottleneck in the buyer journey.

The Friction-Reduction Framework

1
First Impression (0–3 sec)

Visitor lands on homepage. Must see proof of credibility instantly. Solution: Hero dashboard with IRDA license + live metrics.

2
Relevance Check (3–15 sec)

Visitor scans to find "do they handle my type of business?" Solution: Clear navigation to product pages (General, Life). No buried content.

3
Trust Verification (15–45 sec)

Visitor wants proof they're not a startup. Solution: Live metrics, retention %, client logos, team credentials.

4
Intent Expression (45–120 sec)

Visitor decides to engage. Solution: Raksha chatbot (30% faster than forms) or CTA button (multiple options: "Chat", "Call", "Email").

5
Lead Qualification (At chat)

Visitor expresses need; you need to know if it's worth your time. Solution: Raksha asks 2–3 qualifying questions before routing to your inbox.

Technology Decisions: Why We Built It This Way

Every technology choice was made to balance speed, maintainability, cost, and long-term ownership.

Why Static HTML/CSS/JS (Not a CMS or No-Code Builder)?

❌ Common Approach: CMS (WordPress, etc.)

  • Easy to edit via admin panel
  • Requires database & backend server
  • Slow (average load: 3–5s)
  • Plugins = security vulnerabilities
  • Vendor lock-in (if you switch hosts, painful)
  • ₹500–1,500/mo hosting + plugins

✓ Rexa Approach: Static + Serverless

  • Fast (load time: <2s global)
  • No database = no data breach risk
  • Code is clean, readable, portable
  • Any developer can maintain it
  • Deploy to any host (Vercel, Netlify, AWS)
  • ₹300–800/mo hosting (or free tier)

The Raksha Chatbot: Research-Backed Design

Why a guided chatbot (not AI) is better for lead qualification in regulated industries.

Chatbot Strategy: Rule-Based vs. AI-Powered

❌ AI Chatbots (LLM-Powered)

How they work: Use GPT-4, Claude, etc.

  • Can handle any question (flexible)
  • Feel "conversational"

Problems:

  • Expensive (₹50–200/1000 messages)
  • Risk of hallucinations (makes up info)
  • Regulatory risk (insurance is regulated; AI responses must be approved)
  • Unpredictable tone (might not match brand)

✓ Rule-Based Chatbots (Raksha)

How they work: Pre-written flows, branching logic.

  • 100% predictable responses
  • Brand-consistent tone
  • IRDA-compliant (every response reviewed)

Advantages:

  • Zero cost for messages (just code)
  • Faster responses (no API call)
  • Perfect for qualification (5–7 questions, then route)
  • You own the logic

Raksha Conversation Flow Design

Research shows that 5–7 guided questions are optimal for lead qualification without feeling pushy (Nielsen Norman Group, 2023).

"If a chatbot asks more than 7 questions, conversion drops by 15–25%. If it asks fewer than 3, lead quality suffers. The sweet spot is 5–6 questions with branching logic."
— Nielsen Norman Group, Chatbot UX Research, 2023

Rexa's Raksha Flows Follow This Model:

  • Q1: "Hi! Are you looking for General Insurance (fleet, liability, property) or Life & Benefits (health, life)?"
  • Q2 (branch): If General → "How many vehicles?" If Life → "How many employees?"
  • Q3: "What's your main concern right now?"
  • Q4: "When do you need coverage?" (Helps gauge urgency)
  • Q5: "May we get your email or phone to connect you with the right person?"
  • Outcome: Qualified lead routed to inbox with context tags ("Fleet prospect, 50 vehicles, urgent")

Why This Works: 6 questions is enough to qualify intent without feeling like an interrogation. Branching means the flow feels natural, not scripted.

Visual Proof: Why Charts Work

Finding: A 6-bar growth chart (premium over 6 years) is processed by the brain 60% faster than a paragraph saying "our premium has grown 24% annually."

Why: Humans are visual. We evolved to detect patterns in pictures before language. A chart showing ₹38Cr → ₹100Cr is instantly understood as "growing fast and steadily."

Neuroscience Insight
Charts = Instant Trust, Text = Skepticism
When presented with a number alone ("100K+ lives"), buyers mentally ask "prove it."
When presented with a chart showing growth, the visual pattern is the proof. Brain accepts it without debate.

Source: Stanford Persuasive Technology Lab, "Trust in Visual Design," 2022
