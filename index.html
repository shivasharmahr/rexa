How We Work

The Rexa Method.

A structured, audited process designed to deliver clarity and cost optimization at every stage of your risk lifecycle.

Phase 1

Understand Your Needs

We don't sell off-the-shelf policies. We begin with a deep-dive into your business model, physical assets, employee demographics, and existing coverage limits.

Phase 2

Review & Analyse

Our underwriting team conducts in-depth data analysis, identifies hidden exclusions in your current policies, and benchmarks your premiums against market best practices.

Phase 3

Recommend Optimal Solutions

We present unbiased recommendations. We outline the ideal coverage structure, our insurer negotiation strategy, and clear paths for immediate cost optimization.

Phase 4

Implement & Support

We take your risk to market, negotiating hard with 50+ leading insurers. We handle the paperwork, ensuring a smooth policy finalization and digital onboarding for your team.

Phase 5

Continuous Review & Value

Our job starts after the policy is placed. We provide year-round claims advocacy, quarterly utilization reviews, and deploy wellness camps to keep your teams healthy.
