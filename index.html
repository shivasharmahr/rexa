Questions answered

Insurance broking, explained for businesses.

What buyers ask us most about corporate risk, employee health and life cover, and claims support.

What does Rexa Insurance Broking offer?

Rexa is an IRDA-licensed insurance broker offering general insurance (property, liability, marine, engineering), life insurance, and comprehensive employee benefits programmes for businesses across India.

Is Rexa an IRDA-licensed broker?

Yes, Rexa Insurance Broking Services is IRDA-licensed as a direct broker (No. 611) for both life and general insurance since 2017.

How does Rexa help with claims?

Rexa provides end-to-end claims support with a dedicated claims desk, faster resolutions, and strict TAT adherence. We offer 24×7 claims support and work to settle claims promptly.

What is Rexa's client retention rate?

Rexa has a 98% client retention rate, managing 500+ corporate clients and over 100K+ lives covered with ₹100Cr+ in premium managed.

Which industries does Rexa serve?

Rexa serves 17+ industries including manufacturing, IT, construction, healthcare, government, banking, education, hospitality, logistics, retail, energy and real estate sectors.

Does Rexa arrange group health and term life insurance for employees?

Yes. Our employee benefits practice covers group health (GMC), group term life, group personal accident and super top-up health cover, along with wellness programmes and claim-education support for your team.

Can individuals and families buy insurance through Rexa?

Yes. Alongside corporate programmes, Rexa advises on retail cover including motor, health, travel and home insurance, with the same claims support as our business clients.
