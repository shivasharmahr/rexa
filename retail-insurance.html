Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions from individuals looking for personal coverage.

Why should I buy through Rexa instead of directly from an insurer website?
When you buy directly, you have to deal with the insurer's generic customer service during a claim. As an IRDA-licensed broker, Rexa represents you, not the insurance company. We offer unbiased comparisons across multiple insurers, and most importantly, we provide a dedicated claims advocate to fight for your settlement if disputes arise.
Can I port my existing health insurance to a policy recommended by Rexa?
Yes, absolutely. Under IRDAI guidelines, you can port your health insurance policy to another insurer without losing the accumulated benefits like waiting period credits for pre-existing diseases. Our advisors will handle the portability paperwork for you.
What is a Family Floater health insurance plan?
A Family Floater plan covers your entire family (you, spouse, dependent children, and sometimes parents) under a single shared sum insured limit, paying a single premium. It is generally more cost-effective than buying individual policies for every family member.
