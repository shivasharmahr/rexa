Frequently Asked Questions

Common inquiries regarding corporate wellness integration.

Do wellness programs cost extra on top of the insurance premium?
It depends on the scale. Standard health check-up camps and basic mental wellness sessions can often be negotiated directly into your Group Mediclaim (GMC) base premium by our advisors as value-adds. Dedicated OHC setups or dedicated 24/7 ambulance retainers may incur specialized vendor fees, which we negotiate on your behalf.
What is an Occupational Health Center (OHC)?
An OHC is a physical medical facility set up inside your corporate campus or manufacturing plant. Under specific factory acts, large manufacturing units are legally mandated to have an OHC. Rexa helps design the infrastructure, source the medical equipment, and provide certified staff for preventive care and screenings.
