Our Approach

A broker on your side — not the insurer's.

We know the market cold, but we watched clients struggle with paperwork, slow claims, and cover they didn't understand. So we rebuilt the experience around the client — keeping the human expertise, adding the technology that makes it usable.

Today we place risk across general and life lines for businesses of every size, and we manage employee benefits programmes that teams actually use.

What We Value

Principles we won't trade away.

Clarity Over Jargon

If we can't explain a policy plainly, we won't recommend it. No hidden exclusions, no opaque premium structures — we break down the data so you know exactly what you're paying for.

The Client's Side

As brokers, our duty is to you — not to any single insurer. Every recommendation is unbiased and built entirely around your best interests.

Show Up At Claim Time

The real test of insurance is the claim. Our dedicated in-house desk fights relentlessly to ensure fair, timely settlements — that's where we work hardest.

How We Work

The Rexa Method.

A structured, audited process designed to deliver clarity and cost optimisation at every stage of your risk lifecycle.

Phase 01

Understand Your Needs

Deep-dive into your business model, physical assets, employee demographics, and existing coverage limits.

Phase 02

Review & Analyse

In-depth data analysis, gap identification, and benchmarking your premiums against market best practices.

Phase 03

Recommend Solutions

Unbiased recommendations — ideal coverage structure, insurer negotiation strategy, and clear paths for cost optimisation.

Phase 04

Implement & Support

Market approach, negotiation with 50+ leading insurers, and smooth policy finalisation with digital onboarding.

Phase 05

Continuous Review

Post-renewal support, claims advocacy, quarterly utilisation reviews, and wellness camps to keep your teams healthy.
